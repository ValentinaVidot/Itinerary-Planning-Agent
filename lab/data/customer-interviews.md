# Three exploratory interviews

SYNTHETIC CLASSROOM DATA — fixture v1, 2026-09-17. These invented interviews
illustrate conflicting requirements; they are not representative market research.

## INT-SME-01 — owner, six-person online retailer

Q: How do you review customer feedback?
A: We copy comments into a spreadsheet on Fridays. I want a short list of the
main problems, with examples I can open to check. I have no analyst or developer.

Q: What would stop you trying a new product?
A: If I must rename columns or book a setup call, I will put it off. An import
preview that tells me what needs fixing is more useful than another dashboard.
English is enough for this small pilot. I need to finish setup in thirty minutes.

## INT-SME-02 — operations lead, twelve-person service business

Q: What matters most?
A: Saving time. I want email feedback imported automatically. A manual weekly
CSV is acceptable for a short pilot, but not for a permanent workflow. I want
colleagues to comment on findings asynchronously.

Q: What tradeoff would you accept?
A: Fewer charts, if I can see which feedback supports each suggested theme.

## INT-MID-03 — research manager, regional software team

Q: What would make this useful?
A: Separate access for different teams, an audit export, and CRM integration.
We already have an analyst who can prepare CSV files, so guided onboarding is
not our priority. A simple weekly summary is not enough to justify switching.

Q: What evidence is missing?
A: Whether theme suggestions remain accurate on our own terminology.
